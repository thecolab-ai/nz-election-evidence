## Two different inventories

The **24 products** are held metadata from a dated snapshot and sum to 351,710 distinct records. Each `Pxx` appears once in the 52-lane mapping. The **52 lanes** are a research roadmap. A lane may be partial, discovery-only, blocked or not yet available. `52` is not a completeness score.

- Party policy: 17 URLs are not 17 manifestos. The bounded classification was 2 manifestos, 4 collections, 2 platforms and 9 hubs.
- Polling: 12 records; disclosure checked for 9 and unresolved for 3. Unknown is not non-disclosure.
- Candidate finance: 492 document records versus a separate 486-person roster; 224 documents were image-only. Amendments and identity matching are unresolved.
- Party finance: six of 17 originals required bounded vision review over 80 pages; that is not full OCR.
- Written questions: 187,956 snapshot records; independent replay completion had not been certified.
- Bill publications: 115 document records plus 86 catalogue records; four known retrieval gaps.
- RBNZ: 18 catalogue entries, not indicator observations.
- Source bodies and catalogue metadata must not be ranked as equivalent evidence.

No source-derived content is released here, so the counts cannot be queried as substantive facts. They are inventory metadata only.
