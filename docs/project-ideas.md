# High-value, answerable project ideas

1. **Policy-language change log:** Which official policy page wording changed between two archived, rights-safe observations? Requires versioned URLs and precise diffs; absence is not a change.
2. **Scrutiny pathway:** Which topics appear in policies, written questions, committee work and bills? Topic matching is interpretive; volume is not effectiveness.
3. **Legislative status explainer:** How do proposed bills progress? Keep proposed wording distinct from enacted law and record status dates.
4. **Polling disclosure tracker:** Which published polls state sponsor, fieldwork dates, sample and method? Unknown fields remain unknown; do not rank parties.
5. **Regional context cards:** Place electorate results beside Census, rent or benefit indicators only after geography, boundary, period and denominator alignment.
6. **Finance document quality map:** Count embedded-text, image-only, amendment and review states at document level without publishing addresses or inferring influence.
7. **Evidence freshness dashboard:** Show publisher update dates and project observation dates separately; “not recently observed” is not “not published”.
8. **Source rights queue:** Verify exact licence and terms pages, starting with statistical sources, and document what may be redistributed.

Each project should pre-register its question, unit of analysis, denominator, exclusions, uncertainty and non-causal interpretation before producing results.
