# Community Code of Conduct

We are building a respectful, evidence-led, nonpartisan space. Be constructive, welcome correction, critique methods rather than people, disclose uncertainty, and apply standards consistently across political viewpoints.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publishing private or unnecessary personal information, targeted person profiling, partisan campaigning in project spaces, or knowingly misrepresenting evidence.

Report conduct or safety concerns privately through GitHub's repository security/advisory channel or to the organisation owners. Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented where safe, and avoid publicising reporters' identities.
